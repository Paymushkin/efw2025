FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Entry. Are visitor passes free? How do I get an invitation?+
Yes, admission is free, but entry is by invitation only. Invitations can be requested via the VISIT button on our website. Our priority is to welcome buyers (Buyers consist of those purchasing for retail stores as well as for personal use) and fashion/personal stylists, followed by influencers and media representatives. We also invite VIP consumers interested in exclusive designer collections.
Audience. What's the audience profile? How many invites are out?+
As of October 15, 2025, there are 928 registrations for the event. For November 8 and 9, we expect 500+ daily attendees. The audience is diverse, with a majority from beauty, fashion, and creative industries—including influencers (32.8%), fashion stylists (25.6%), bloggers (19.6%), media representatives (16.4%), buyers (37.7%), and others (7.7%). Participants range from industry professionals to fashion-conscious individuals across the Middle East, CIS, Eastern Europe, and UAE.
Market. What is a popup market / popup corner / spot? When does it take place?+
The popup market is a special daytime event held after the main fashion shows (November 10–11, Monday and Tuesday). It features 40 designer collections presented on the runway during the show days, which can be explored, tried on, and purchased. In addition, there are 60 showcase spots for beauty service providers—including nails, hair, skincare, cosmetology, fitness, wellness, clinics, dental studios, salons, and more.
Beauty Services. What are some example brands in the business showcase corner?+
Over 30 brands are confirmed — including designers from the Middle East, CIS, Eastern Europe, and local UAE talents. We also host a wide range of beauty service providers: clinics, plastic surgery, botox, fitness, cosmetology, nails, lashes, brows, hair, and styling.
Dates. 8-9 November: Runways vs 10-11 November: Pop-up & Beauty Market+
On November 8–9 we'll have the evening runway shows — so the audience will be much larger, but they'll be mainly focused on watching the shows continuously. There will be less time and space for visitors to stop by the pop-ups in the corridors. On November 10–11, however, the audience will have the entire day (10 AM – 6 PM) to explore all the stands calmly — to touch the clothes, discover brands, and interact with designers and beauty services. So: 8–9 November: bigger audience, but you'll be competing for attention with the entertainment and runway program. 10–11 November: smaller but more attentive audience, fully dedicated to visiting the market. Also, on November 8–9 we'll feature only about 10–15 brands in the corridors to avoid blocking guest flow, while on November 10–11 we expect around 60 beauty services and 20–40 fashion corners in the main market area.
Fakes Policy+
We've identified fraudulent accounts and websites impersonating Emirates Fashion Week. These fake profiles attempt to mislead designers and the public by copying our name and visuals. Please note that our only official pages are @emirates_fashionweek on Instagram (black logo with blue verified badge) and our website www.emiratesfashionweeks.com. We never request payments via links or personal messages. If you see suspicious activity, report it immediately and verify any event information through our authentic channels. Your safety and trust matter — always follow and tag only our verified accounts for official updates and participation details.
LeadGen. What is the AI matchmaking tool? How does it work?+
The AI matchmaking tool is available on laptops at each exhibitor spots. Organizers will introduce at least 50 visitor leads to every exhibitor through chat. Some visitors will approach the station directly, while others will be connected via chat for online introductions and follow-ups.

Emirates Fashion Week® — Official Fashion Week in Dubai

Emirates Fashion Week® is an international fashion and beauty platform, founded in 2019 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, hosting biannual fashion weeks in the Spring/Summer (SS) and Autumn/Winter (AW) seasons.

The official website is www.emiratesfashionweek.com

Official social media:

  • Instagram — @emirates_fashionweek
  • YouTube — https://www.youtube.com/@EmiratesFashionWeek

Emirates Fashion Week® is an independent fashion week and is not affiliated with emiratesfashionweek.org.

A Leading Fashion & Beauty Platform in the GCC

Emirates Fashion Week® is a major platform for promoting designers, fashion brands and beauty companies in Dubai and the GCC region, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman.

We connect:

  • international and regional designers
  • fashion and beauty brands
  • stylists, hair professionals and makeup artists
  • buyers, retailers and distributors
  • media, influencers and celebrities

Formats Within Emirates Fashion Week®

▪️ Fashion Shows — Runway shows presenting designer collections.

▪️ Fashion Stylist Shows — Curated runway concepts created by professional fashion stylists.

▪️ Beauty & Hair Fashion Shows — Unique runway formats for hair stylists, barbers and makeup artists, allowing beauty brands and professionals to showcase their artistry, techniques and products.

▪️ Designer Showrooms — Private showrooms offering direct access to:

  • buyers
  • multibrand stores
  • investors and distributors

▪️ Pop-Up Market — A pop-up space where fashion and beauty brands:

  • present new collections and products
  • communicate directly with buyers, celebrities and guests
  • launch sales during and immediately after Fashion Week

Emirates Fashion Week Magazine®

Emirates Fashion Week® publishes Emirates Fashion Week Magazine, available in:

  • print edition — 5,000 copies
  • digital online version

The magazine features designers, industry leaders, fashion trends and exclusive interviews.

Education & Professional Development

Emirates Fashion Week® hosts:

  • professional conferences
  • masterclasses
  • educational programs for fashion and beauty professionals

Designer Residency & Market Entry

Resident designers of Emirates Fashion Week® receive:

  • retail corners in multibrand stores
  • opportunities to open their own boutiques
  • immediate sales launch after participation
  • access to the UAE and GCC fashion market

Awards & Recognition

As part of Emirates Fashion Week®, an official awards ceremony is held to recognize outstanding professionals in the fashion and beauty industry.

Awards are presented in the following categories:

  • Best Designer of the Year
  • Best Fashion Stylist of the Year
  • Best Hair Stylist of the Year
  • Best Makeup Artist of the Year

The awards highlight creative excellence, professional achievement and contribution to the fashion and beauty industry in Dubai and the GCC region.
